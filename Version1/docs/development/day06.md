## 3. Main Architectural Direction

The estate design was established with the following characteristics:

- Massive sprawling ancestral property.
- Traditional Kerala architectural identity.
- Approximately 250-year-old appearance.
- Two-floor main mansion.
- Partially abandoned condition.
- Multiple connected wings.
- Central open courtyard.
- Large layered tiled roof.
- Laterite stone and dark teak wood.
- Dense tropical surroundings.

The mansion should look like an old family estate that has experienced multiple generations of changes and renovations.

## 4. Central Courtyard — Nadumuttam

The central courtyard is the architectural heart of the mansion.

| Property | Decision |
|---|---|
| Shape | Rectangular |
| Width | 16 Godot units |
| Depth | 12 Godot units |
| Area | 192 square units |
| Position | Exact centre |
| Roof | Completely open to sky |
| Surroundings | Four connected wings |
| Upper level | Wooden gallery on all four sides |

Area calculation:

16 × 12 = 192 square units.

The courtyard will allow:

- Direct rainfall.
- Natural daylight.
- Moonlight during nighttime.
- Water collection and drainage.
- Reflections and supernatural events.

The upper gallery must not cover the open courtyard.

## 13. The Thirteenth Room

The thirteenth room will remain separate from the twelve documented rooms.

It must not appear as an ordinary permanent room in the initial architectural plan.

Its appearance and access will be designed through the supernatural story mechanics later.

Possible behaviours:

- Appearing in unexpected locations.
- Changing its interior.
- Disappearing.
- Connecting to memories.
- Containing objects linked to the protagonist.

These are planned gameplay possibilities, not yet implemented features.
