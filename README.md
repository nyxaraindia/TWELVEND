# TWELVEND

<sub>Psychological horror mystery • Godot 4.7 • Narrative-driven exploration</sub>

TWELVEND is a psychological horror mystery game set in a house with twelve rooms, each carrying the weight of silence, memory, and dread. But behind the familiar arrangement of walls and doors, a thirteenth room appears—an impossible space that hides a truth no one is meant to remember.

This project blends atmospheric exploration, environmental storytelling, and a slow unraveling mystery into a tense, immersive experience designed to unsettle players through both the architecture of the house and the fragments of the past it contains.

## Overview

The player enters a decaying home that appears to follow rational rules at first glance, only to discover that the house is not behaving like a normal space. The deeper they explore, the more the boundaries between reality, memory, and guilt begin to break apart.

Each room is a piece of a larger narrative puzzle. Some hold traces of previous occupants. Others shift in ways that resist logic. The final truth is not simply hidden in the walls—it is embedded in the emotional fracture at the center of the house itself.

## Core Experience

- Explore a haunted, psychologically warped house
- Discover 12 rooms and the impossible 13th room
- Uncover hidden lore through environment, text, and narrative fragments
- Piece together a mystery rooted in trauma, memory, and loss
- Experience a tense atmosphere built around dread, uncertainty, and revelation

## Story Premise

The house has twelve rooms by design. Yet there is always one more.

The thirteenth room should not exist. It does not follow the same laws as the rest of the house, and it appears only to those who are ready—or doomed—to confront what the house has been hiding. As the player moves through the structure, they begin to uncover a story that suggests the rooms are not merely physical spaces, but emotional echoes of people who lived, suffered, and vanished within them.

The house is not simply haunted. It remembers.

## Narrative Direction

TWELVEND is built around the idea that horror is strongest when it is personal. Rather than relying on spectacle alone, the project emphasizes:

- Psychological tension over jump-scare pacing
- Environmental storytelling over exposition-heavy cutscenes
- Discovery-driven progression rooted in emotional truth
- A mystery that rewards attention, interpretation, and patient investigation

## Technical Stack

TWELVEND is being developed in Godot Engine and is configured for a mobile-friendly presentation while maintaining a polished, atmospheric game workflow.

- Engine: Godot 4.7
- Rendering: Mobile-oriented rendering setup
- Physics: Jolt Physics
- Platform focus: Mobile-ready development environment

## Development Status

This repository currently contains the early game foundation and narrative materials for Version 1, including the project configuration and the underlying story structure. The project is positioned as a narrative-first horror experience with room-based exploration and mystery progression as central pillars.

## Why TWELVEND?

TWELVEND aims to capture the feeling of being trapped in a place where every door leads somewhere slightly more wrong than before. It is a story about unfinished memory, impossible architecture, and the terrifying notion that the house itself may know more than the person trying to escape it.

## Getting Started

1. Clone the repository.
2. Open the project in Godot 4.7.
3. Launch the `Version1` project.
4. Explore the house, investigate the room layouts, and begin uncovering the truth behind the thirteenth room.
